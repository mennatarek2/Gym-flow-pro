Verdict

Yes — add Cancel on the membership card. There is no cancel API today. The only way a plan becomes cancelled is a full sale refund. That is money. Desk also needs “this person should not train on this plan anymore.”

Cancel is not Refund. Cancel stops check-in and invitations on this covering period. Cash already taken stays in the gym. Remaining unpaid on this plan is not collected. If they should get money back, use Refund on the sale (existing).
Cancel is not Archive. The left-column Active toggle is the person account. Leave it alone. A cancelled plan can still belong to an Active person (Renew / Assign next).
Cancel is not Freeze. Freeze is a hold with a return date. Cancel is historical. Do not resurrect the cancelled row. Renew or Assign starts a new membership — same as after a full refund.

What happens

  • Membership.Status = cancelled. Dates stay. History keeps the row.
  • Check-in and invitation remaining on this period = 0 (covering is gone).
  • If this plan’s sale is still outstanding (paid 100 of 300), remaining is no longer collected. That is not a refund and not a write-off screen. Sale is not marked refunded.
  • Pending Fawry: cancel the pending membership. No gateway refund (unsupported).
  • Lime stays on Renew. Cancel is a quiet danger control. Confirm in a dialog.
  • Who: Manager or above (same as Assign / Renew). Do not add a new role. Do not reuse payments.refund.*

Do not

  • Put Collect Payment on this card.
  • Auto-refund cash or create a credit note.
  • Restore Refunds as a nav desk.
  • Add Member.DebtAmount.
  • Treat person Archive as membership cancel.

Implemented on Member 360. POST /api/memberships/{memberId}/cancel. Cache member-detail.js?v=ms-cancel1. Not a refund.
